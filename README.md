# Small Hours

**Open it here: https://ishakbou.github.io/small-hours/**

A house for the late part of the night. Seven rooms of small, free tools: the real sky, real recorded sounds, moving scenes, stories read aloud, prayer times and calm.

- **The Roof**: a real star map poster for any night and place, the moon tonight, tonight's sky, a day-and-night map of the world.
- **Listening Room**: a mixer of ten real recorded sounds (rain, storm, wind, sea, fire, café, birds, stream, crickets, train), **The Window** (moving scenes with a clock and a timer), world radio with search and saved stations, endless music, a candle.
- **Quiet Room**: breathing, grounding, burn your worries, a meditation bell, one line a day, and **Sleep**: a dim red clock while the sound fades, then a gentle sunrise with birdsong to wake you.
- **The Library**: nineteen stories (long tales from the Thousand and One Nights, fairy tales, fables, and sleep stories) and nineteen poems in English, French and Arabic, read aloud with the words lighting up, and a word of the night to collect.
- **The Desk**: a study garden timer, flashcards, exam countdowns, focus mode, a memory palace.
- **The Courtyard**: prayer times for your place, a qibla compass, the Hijri calendar and a Ramadan countdown.
- **The Doorstep**: the sound of a name, a sealed letter, a moon postcard, and greeting cards for Eid, Ramadan, birthdays and more, in Arabic, English or French.

Outside the house a quiet night plays; it stops when you walk into a room. What you play stays in its room unless you press **Follow me**. On a computer, two rooms can sit side by side (the **Two rooms** button in the player bar).

Free, no account, nothing tracked. Everything is saved only on the device. Works offline once opened (only the radio needs internet). Installs like an app on phones and computers.

## Use it on this computer

Open the folder and double-click **index.html**. Everything works straight from the folder, with no internet, except the radio. To install it as an app with its own icon, put it online first (below).

## Put it online for free (GitHub Pages)

1. Make a free account at github.com.
2. Click **New repository**. Name it `small-hours`, choose **Public**, click **Create repository**.
3. Click **uploading an existing file**, drag in everything inside this folder (not the folder itself, and you can leave out `src`, `build.sh` and `gen-sw.mjs`), then click **Commit changes**.
4. Open **Settings › Pages**. Under *Branch*, choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then **Save**.
5. Wait a minute or two. Your house is at `https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/small-hours/`.
