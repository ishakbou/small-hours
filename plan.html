The House

The Plan of the House

Seven rooms of small tools for the late part of the night.

open nowstill being built

In every room

  • Free. No account. No ads. Nothing tracked.
  • Everything you make stays on your own device. Settings › Backup moves it to a new phone.
  • Works with no internet once opened. Only the radio reaches outside.
  • Outside the house, a quiet night plays: crickets and a little wind. It stops when you walk into a room, so each room’s own sounds can be heard. The little house in the player turns it off.
  • What you play stays in its room. Press Follow me in the player to take it with you.
  • Install it like an app on phone or computer: how.

Made with

  • Moon, planets and rise times: Astronomy Engine by Don Cross (MIT).
  • Star positions and constellation lines: d3-celestial data by Olaf Frohn (BSD-3), from the Hipparcos catalogue.
  • City names and places: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0).
  • Radio stations: the open Radio Browser directory. Streams come from the stations themselves.
  • Prayer times and the qibla: Adhan by Batoul Apps (MIT). Hijri dates from the Umm al-Qura calendar built into your browser.
  • World outlines: world-atlas from Natural Earth (ISC; public domain data).
  • Type: IM Fell English, VT323, IBM Plex Mono, Cormorant Garamond, Nanum Pen Script, Courier Prime, Special Elite, Libre Baskerville, EB Garamond, Goudy Bookletter 1911, Amiri and Noto Kufi Arabic (SIL Open Font License).
  • The Library: the stories are Small Hours’ own retellings of old tales that belong to everyone (the Brothers Grimm, the Jataka tales, Hans Christian Andersen, Juha, and Ibn al-Muqaffa’s Kalila wa Dimna). The poems are the poets’ own words, all long in the public domain. Reading aloud uses the voices already on your device.
